\chapter{Ethical and Fairness Considerations}

\section{Fairness Constraints}

BED enforces exposure fairness:


\[
|\mathbb{E}[a_t | g=i] - \mathbb{E}[a_t | g=j]| \le \epsilon.
\]



\section{Privacy}

The prototype collects no personally identifiable information (PII).
All events are synthetic and anonymized.

\section{Responsible Deployment}

BED should be deployed with:
\begin{itemize}
    \item transparent incentive policies,
    \item clear user consent,
    \item fairness auditing,
    \item safeguards against over-incentivization.
\end{itemize}
