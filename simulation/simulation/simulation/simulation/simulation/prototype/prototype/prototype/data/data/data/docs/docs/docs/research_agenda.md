# BED v3 — Research Agenda (Public Version)

This document outlines the forward-looking research agenda for the Behavioral
Engine for Demand (BED) v3. It is designed to guide academic collaboration and
future development while keeping all proprietary extensions private.

---

## 1. Behavioral Modeling Extensions

- Expand habit and recency dynamics with multi-timescale memory.
- Introduce heterogeneity across synthetic user segments.
- Explore alternative hazard models (e.g., spline-based, neural hazard).

---

## 2. CMDP Policy Optimization

- Evaluate alternative constraint formulations (fairness, budget, pacing).
- Compare Lagrangian vs. primal-dual vs. approximate dynamic programming.
- Study robustness under model misspecification.

---

## 3. Simulation Experiments

- Stress-test BED under extreme synthetic demand patterns.
- Conduct ablation studies on behavioral components.
- Evaluate sensitivity to parameter drift.

---

## 4. Prototype Development

- Expand QR → Web → Cloud Function → Firestore loop.
- Add synthetic incentive types and response patterns.
- Explore offline-first and low-bandwidth variants.

---

## 5. Pilot Design (Synthetic)

- Extend MIT campus micro-pilot to 10–20 synthetic nodes.
- Introduce time-of-day and day-of-week patterns.
- Evaluate policy performance under synthetic congestion scenarios.

---

## 6. Ethical and Practical Considerations

- Study incentive fatigue and fairness constraints.
- Explore transparency and user-control mechanisms.
- Document limitations of synthetic evaluation.

---

## 7. Future Work (Private)

The following items are intentionally excluded from the public agenda:

- Real-world deployment plans
- Production integrations
- Proprietary tuning logic
- Commercialization pathways

These remain private and are not part of the public repository.

---

This agenda provides a structured roadmap for academic collaboration and
continued development of BED v3.
