# BED v3 Roadmap

This roadmap outlines planned milestones for the BED v3 research program.

## Phase 1 — Public Release (Complete)
- Simulation engine
- Prototype architecture
- Synthetic MIT micro-pilot
- Documentation and research agenda

## Phase 2 — Extended Simulation
- Multi-segment behavioral heterogeneity
- Multi-timescale habit and recency
- Robustness and sensitivity analysis

## Phase 3 — Advanced CMDP Research
- Fairness constraints
- Budget pacing
- Lagrangian tuning (private)

## Phase 4 — Prototype Expansion
- Additional synthetic incentive types
- Offline-first variant
- Low-bandwidth mode

## Phase 5 — Synthetic Pilot Scaling
- 10–20 synthetic MIT nodes
- Time-of-day patterns
- Congestion simulation

## Phase 6 — Private Extensions (Not Public)
- Real-world deployment planning
- Production integrations
- Commercialization pathways
